# When/Now UX Test Script

Use this for quick informal testing. The goal is not to explain the app. The goal is to see where people naturally get stuck.

App link: https://gatherround-scheduler.atropea677558.chatgpt.site/

## Copy/Paste Invite

Hi! I am testing a scheduling poll app called When/Now and would love 5-10 minutes of your help.

Please try the link below without me explaining how it works. As you use it, notice anything that feels confusing, annoying, surprisingly easy, or missing.

App: https://gatherround-scheduler.atropea677558.chatgpt.site/

When you are done, tap the Feedback button in the app and leave a quick note. Brutal honesty is welcome.

## Test Tasks

1. Create a scheduling poll for a recurring meeting.
2. Add at least three available times using days of the week and times.
3. Copy the attendee link.
4. Open the attendee link in another browser, private window, or different device.
5. Respond to the poll as an attendee.
6. Return to My polls as the organizer.
7. Finalize a chosen time.
8. Open the finalized poll link and find the calendar options.
9. Send feedback using the Feedback button.

## Watch For

- Could they tell where to start?
- Did Create, My polls, and Respond mean what they expected?
- Did they understand attendee link versus admin link?
- Did recurring days of week make sense?
- Did the final time feel obvious after finalizing?
- Did they find Google Calendar and calendar invite options?
- Did anything feel too loud, too hidden, too wordy, or too clever?

## Follow-Up Questions

1. What did you think the app was for before you clicked anything?
2. Where, if anywhere, did you hesitate?
3. What wording felt unclear?
4. What would you expect to happen after a time is finalized?
5. Would you use this instead of a group text, email thread, or Doodle-style poll?
6. What is one thing you would remove?
7. What is one thing you would add only if it stayed simple?

## Notes For The Tester

This is an early public beta. Please do not use it for sensitive meetings yet. Poll links are public to anyone who has the link, and admin links should be kept private.
